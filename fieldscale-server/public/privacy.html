Privacy Policy

How Fieldscale handles your data.

What we collect

Fieldscale stores only what's needed to run your business in the app:

  • Account info — your username and a securely hashed password (we never store your password in plain text).
  • Business records — the leads, customers, estimates, invoices, jobs, price book, employees, subcontractors and documents you enter.
  • Basic usage — sign-in times and activity for your own security log.

Data minimization

We collect only what the features you use require. We do not ask for or store payment card numbers — online payments go through your own payment provider's secure link. Public forms (like the "Request a Quote" form) capture only the fields a visitor fills in.

How your data is used

Your data is used solely to provide the app to you and your company. Each company's data is walled off from every other company. We do not sell your data or share it with third parties for advertising.

Security

Passwords are salted and hashed, sessions are signed, traffic is encrypted over HTTPS, and standard browser security protections are in place. Data is backed up automatically. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information.

Your control

Owners and admins can export a full backup of their data at any time from the Team page, and can delete records or accounts. Contact your Fieldscale administrator to request deletion of your data.

Contact

Questions about your data? Contact the company that provides your Fieldscale account.
